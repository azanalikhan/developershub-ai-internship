DevelopersHub Corporation — Internal Knowledge Base (Sample Corpus)

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local timezone.
Remote employees must attend the weekly all-hands meeting on Mondays at 9:30 AM.

## Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue 18 paid leave days per year, plus 10 public holidays.
Sick leave of up to 3 consecutive days does not require a medical certificate.
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days
in advance, except for emergencies.

## Onboarding Process
New hires complete a 2-week onboarding program covering company tools, security
training, and role-specific shadowing. IT provisions laptops and accounts within
the first 48 hours. A dedicated onboarding buddy is assigned for the first month.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $50 can be self-approved by submitting a receipt through
the finance portal. Expenses above $50 require manager approval before submission.
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days.

## AI/ML Internship Program
The AI/ML Engineering Internship consists of foundational and advanced task sets.
Interns must complete at least 3 of 5 advanced tasks, covering transformer
fine-tuning, ML pipelines, multimodal learning, conversational AI with RAG, and
LLM-based ticket tagging. All submissions require a GitHub repository with a
README describing objective, methodology, and results.

## IT Support
The IT helpdesk is available Monday-Friday, 9 AM-6 PM, via the #it-support Slack
channel. Password resets are self-service through the SSO portal. Hardware
issues should be logged as a ticket with priority level and device asset tag.
